Final Approach

Three agents agree a safe revised arrival when disruption hits

Autonomous SG 2026 · Airbus Challenge 2 · Air traffic decisions

The problem

A delayed arrival needs a new runway, slot and gate.

Today the airline duty manager phones the tower, the crew and ground handling, one call at a time. Nobody checks the combined answer against wake, fuel, crew-duty and gate rules together.

How it works

ATCrunway, slot, order
PILOTspeed, ground delay, divert
GROUNDgates, remote stand

One structured message per agent per turn, at most 3 turns. No agreement: a deterministic solver decides and logs why.

Safety by construction

A 12-rule engine decides legality. No model ever does.

Wake separation, runway capacity, FAA crosswind limits, fuel reserve, crew duty, gate size and availability. Every veto cites the rule, the source and the numbers.

Code does the maths, the model judges

TypeSafe jev-1.14 picks moves and votes on pre-computed verdicts.

“Fixes 2 rule breaches”, “clearly better for you”, “meets your minimum”. Consensus rose from 68–94% to 98% once raw numbers left the prompt. Claude Haiku 5.5 writes the duty-manager brief and decides nothing.

Honest about the data

Flights, delays, runways, weather and gates are real.

DOT 2015 flights.csv, OurAirports runways, IEM METAR, OpenStreetMap gates. Gate occupancy, fuel, crew shifts and connections are simulated and badged. Every limit used is returned with its source.
